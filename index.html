The top 100 Jewel holders split the pool, but the payout table is banded. Fifteen players can hold fifteen different Jewel counts and be paid exactly the same amount. Climbing inside a band pays nothing. Only crossing a band line does. Enter your Jewels and this reads the live leaderboard to tell you where the nearest line is and what it costs to get there.

Where the numbers come from

Pool size, global Jewel count and the full top 100 come from the game's own campaign data, read off the Sack panel and written into this page by hand. The board is a snapshot, not a live feed. The game's endpoint needs a logged-in session, so a public page cannot refresh it automatically. The band shares are the same ones the game returns as rewardShareBps.

Paid in VALOR, not dollars

VALOR is pegged at 100 VALOR to 1 USDC and is tracked off-chain rather than as a token. Cashing out carries a 5% withdrawal fee, a 500 VALOR minimum and a 24 hour delay. The payout figure is before that fee. The USDC figure is after it.

Projected, not final

Your rank is worked out by comparing your Jewels against the top 100 in the snapshot above. Everyone else is still playing, so a Jewel count that holds a band today will not hold it in a week. The pool also grows with every Expedition Pass sold, so payouts here are a floor rather than a forecast.
